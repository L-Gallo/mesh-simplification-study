3D Mesh Quality Comparison Study

Breda University of Applied Sciences

Instructions

You will be shown pairs of 3D mesh images at different viewing distances. For each pair, please choose the image that you think has better overall visual quality.

  • Some images will appear smaller (as if viewed from a distance in a game)
  • Some images will appear larger (close-up view)
  • There are no right or wrong answers - we want your honest opinion
  • Take your time to examine both images carefully
  • Each session includes max 20 comparisons (approximately 4-5 minutes)
  • You can return later to complete more comparisons if you wish
  • You can exit at any time - each answer is saved immediately

Data Privacy: This study only records which image you prefer and the time taken to decide. No personal information, IP addresses, or identifying data is collected. Your participation is anonymous and voluntary.
